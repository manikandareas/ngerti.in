## Acceptance still open

The versioned baseline contains **27 surfaces and 307 named surface/state pairs**, expanded to **3,684 configurations** by three viewports, two themes and two scales. This cohort contains focused observations and regressions; it is not an executed 3,684-case comparison. No percentage score substitutes for missing states. See the [surface coverage and difference register](differences.md) for exact gaps and reproduction.

The complete matched cold/warm startup, streaming/follow/history, large tree/source/table/PDF and 1/10/30 visible-avatar workloads remain unexecuted in this cohort. Frame-time, missed-frame, latency and Tauri/Rust memory-ratio budgets therefore remain open. The single confirmed hidden sample does not close that gate.

Earlier background/occluded observation attempts showed accessibility data advancing while the raster stayed on Connecting. Normal Finder Open subsequently passed with retained and fresh isolated profiles, without geometry changes or refresh actions. The two ineffective first-snapshot refresh trials were removed. The sheet's separate long-roster/footer overflow was reproduced and corrected with a bounded body and a stronger regression test. Native window geometry now persists viewport dimensions rather than AppKit frame dimensions; the actual relaunch comparison passed at the recorded fixed display/geometry.

Additional native IME/composition, abrupt process termination during upload/unknown external effects, complete integrated Goals/Routine/Settings/apps/memory/skills/file journeys and deterministic animation timestamps still need the specified retained evidence. Earlier milestone component/host tests remain useful evidence for their recorded revisions; they are not silently recounted as this milestone's full integrated acceptance.
