## Objective

2. Exercise private chat/memory/derived files, published knowledge/skills, personal/organization AI and app connections, and organization/server/account switching with retained state.
3. Exercise queue/steer/stop/HITL, scheduled responsibilities, revoke/remove, in-flight effects, viewer transfer, server restart and lost acknowledgement.

5. Record observed latency/resource capacity on a declared concurrent workload, unresolved limits and adoption/upgrade notes; reconcile all scope and acceptance documents.

The integrated actor matrix uses two organizations, three people with distinct roles/Workgroups, a cross-organization account and an outsider. Real API desktop/mobile and actual Tauri journeys retain private Team interactions, explicit publication, scoped connections, switching/drafts, revocation and client lifecycle. Separate deployed Codex/Muse file/Computer workflows, HTTPS/SSE/WSS/isolation probes and a native Routine completed while the actual client was Quit preserve their own receipts.
